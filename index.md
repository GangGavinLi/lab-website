# Gang Li Research Lab

Advancing AI for energy resiliency and sustainability, condition monitoring, predictive maintenance, and advanced mechanical design — bridging engineering innovation with entrepreneurial impact.

{% include section.html %}

## Research Focus

{% capture text %}

Dr. Gang Li leads the Lab for AI and Energy Resilience at the Michael W. Hall School of Mechanical Engineering, Mississippi State University. His research integrates artificial intelligence, renewable energy technologies, condition monitoring algorithms, predictive maintenance, vibration and control theory, and life cycle assessment to develop more resilient and sustainable energy infrastructure.

Dr. Li is a recipient of the NSF EPSCoR Research Fellow Award and the DOE EnergyTech UP Faculty Explorer Award. His research has been supported by NSF, DOE, VentureWell, the Maryland Energy Innovation Institute, TEDCO, the Maryland Offshore Wind Energy Research Challenge Grant Program, General Electric, and Baltimore Gas and Electric. He is a member of ASME, IEEE, and SAE International, and serves as an Early Career Editorial Board Member for *Results in Engineering* and *Journal of Dynamics and Control*.
